# The Evidence Base

Research run August 2026. Every claim below carries a source. Where the research **contradicts** something in this plan, that is marked — those are the findings worth re-reading.

Re-run this whole file every 90 days. Benchmarks move.

---

## 1. Is the market saturated?

**No — but the entry level is.**

The AI automation market is projected to grow from **$169.5B in 2026 to $1,144.8B by 2033, a 31.4% CAGR** ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/ai-automation-market-report)).

The more useful finding is the split: *"A market adding billions in demand a year is not full — it is just full of undifferentiated supply at the entry level."* The crowded top competes on price against clones; the wide-open bottom is populated by operators who are the only AI person a local business has ever met ([Ciela](https://ciela.ai/blogs/is-ai-agency-market-saturated-reddit)).

And the gap that matters most: **in any given business function, no more than 10% of organisations say they are actually *scaling* AI agents — the rest are piloting.**

> **What this means here:** the opportunity is not building agents. It's being the person who gets them past the pilot. That is an implementation and enablement problem, which is what Exponential World already sells.

## 2. What kills businesses like this?

**40% of AI startups fail within 24 months.** Of 14,000+ AI startups launched globally in 2024, 3,800 shut down in 2025 (27%) and another 1,800 closed in early 2026 ([Pertama Partners](https://www.pertamapartners.com/insights/ai-project-failure-statistics-2026)).

**Over 80% of AI projects fail — roughly twice the rate of conventional IT projects. About 95% of generative AI pilots deliver no measurable P&L return.**

> **What this means here:** the 30-day rule — no agent enters the product until it has run this business for a month — is not caution, it is the differentiator. In a market where 95% of pilots produce nothing measurable, "I've been running this on my own business for 30 days and here are the numbers" is the entire pitch.

## 3. The single most important finding: in-housing

**AI-driven in-housing is the defining agency churn factor of 2026.** Agencies positioned primarily as *execution providers* lose clients to in-house teams equipped with AI tools. Agencies that lead with **strategic direction and proprietary methodology** retain at significantly higher rates ([Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/average-marketing-agency-churn/)).

The defence, from firms actually doing it ([AI Smart Ventures](https://aismartventures.com/posts/how-ai-is-changing-the-agency-strategy-retainer-in-2026/), [Arete](https://arete.so/blog/ai-customer-retention-pr-agencies)):

- Reposition as a **strategic AI partner** — teach the client's team, recommend tooling, build hybrid in-house/agency workflows
- Sell **strategic judgment and relationship capital**, not deliverable production
- Build **AI governance and enablement** into the retainer as a benefit, not a separate SKU
- Firms that made this shift report **28% lower voluntary churn**

> **What this means here — and it reframes the whole business:** Paras is a *trainer who installs*, not an agency that executes. That looked like a weakness ("he's not technical enough to be a real automation shop"). The 2026 data says it is the durable position. **Teaching the client's team to run it is the moat, not the top of the funnel.**

Supporting economics: **retainer-based agencies hold clients ~56 months versus ~24 months for project-based**. The retainer half of the installed-OS offer is not an upsell — it is the business.

## 4. Does build-in-public actually work?

Yes, and by a wide margin.

- **Founder inbound converts at 14.6% vs 1.7% for outbound** — roughly 8.6× ([Workflows.io](https://www.workflows.io/blog/founder-led-marketing))
- Personal profiles generate **7× the impressions** of company pages
- Founders building in public have **replaced the company page as the highest-converting B2B inbound channel in 2026** ([HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/startups/sales-and-marketing/founder-led-content-strategy))
- It is described as the highest trust-to-effort ratio content available, because buyers have lost trust in faceless institutions and AI-generated content

**The finding that changes the staffing plan:** teams where **five or more leadership voices post consistently generate 3–5× more organic reach** than organisations relying on a single founder's voice.

> **What this means here:** the key-person risk and the reach ceiling have the same fix. Get the OS Operator, the ads manager and the VA posting under their own names about the build. It costs nothing, multiplies reach 3–5×, and starts decoupling the brand from one person.

## 5. Webinar and launch benchmarks

For sizing Sprint 0 ([Aevent](https://aevent.com/average-webinar-conversion-rate/), [Presentr](https://presentr.co.uk/learn/webinar-conversion-rate-benchmarks), [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/webinar-statistics-2026-attendance-conversion-data)):

| Stage | Benchmark |
|---|---|
| Registration → live attendance | Median **41.6%** (bottom quartile 28.4%, top quartile 55.2%) |
| Attendee → offer, educational | 15–25% |
| Attendee → offer, high-ticket coaching | **5–12%** |
| Attendee → offer, sales webinar | 10–20% |
| On-webinar CTA conversion | ~22–25%, with **two CTAs performing best** |
| Registration → revenue, paid traffic | 2–5% |

**Interactive polls, live chat and pop-up offers lift CTA click rates up to 3×, and engaged attendees are 30% more likely to convert.**

> **What this means here:** the existing 46-slide deck already runs six poll beats and demo-before-offer. The retention mechanism the benchmarks credit with a 3× lift **is already built.** Sprint 0 is not building a webinar — it is running one that exists, more often.

## 6. Creator-economy monetisation, India

- India has **2M+ monetised creators**, with growth *"concentrated in owned, recurring businesses built around communities rather than reach, platform virality, or brand deals"* ([OwnStreet](https://ownstreet.in/blog/creator-economy-india-2026-guide))
- A cohort priced **₹3,000–15,000 sold to 100–1,000 students generates ₹3L–1.5Cr per launch**
- A single course launch to a loyal **100K-subscriber** audience is reported at **₹50L–2Cr** ([IdentityKit](https://www.identitykit.in/blog/youtube-monetization-india-2026))

> **Caveat, stated plainly:** these are reported upper ranges from creator-economy sources with an incentive to be optimistic. Discount them hard. Even at a quarter of the low end, adjusted down for a smaller subscriber base, **one launch plausibly clears a month or more of current revenue** — and the asset already exists.

## 7. Pricing benchmarks

**Corporate AI training, India** ([BuildFastWithAI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/corporate-ai-training-india-guide)):

| Engagement | Price |
|---|---|
| Per employee | ₹1,200–12,000 |
| One-day awareness workshop, 100 people | **₹1.5L–4L** |
| Multi-week role-specific programme, 100 people | **₹8L–25L** |

**AI automation / consulting, global SMB** ([Taskip](https://taskip.net/ai-automation-agency-pricing/), [LeanStrat](https://leanstrat.co/blog/ai-consulting-pricing-guide-2026), [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agency-services-pricing-strategies-2026)):

| Model | Price |
|---|---|
| SMB setup fee | $500–3,000 one-time |
| SMB monthly retainer | $300–1,500 |
| Median retainer, small–midmarket | $2,800–7,000/mo |
| Agent-licence model | $20K setup + $2K/mo |
| SMB consulting hourly | $100–250 |
| Project-based | $5,000–80,000 |
| SMB decision cycle | **2–6 weeks** |

**The hybrid model — fixed-fee build plus a monthly retainer — is the 2026 default for SMB**, because it balances cost certainty against continuous evolution.

> **What this means here:** the Nippon India MF engagement was a two-day AI training programme. That is a ₹1.5–4L+ shape at benchmark, and a multi-week version is ₹8–25L. **One corporate engagement equals a month or more of current revenue, and the proof to sell it already exists.** Corporate training is not the low-value top of the funnel — it is the highest-ticket, fastest-closing thing on the menu.

## 8. SEBI — correcting an earlier error

The v1 plan framed SEBI registration as a heavy compliance workstream. **That is now wrong.**

- SEBI **eased the criteria in 2026**: graduates of *any* discipline qualify for Investment Adviser and Research Analyst registration, and the **prior-experience requirement has been removed** ([Taxation Consultancy](https://taxationconsultancy.in/sebi-eases-for-ia-and-ra/), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/sebi-eases-norms-for-boarding-of-investment-advisors-research-analysts-124121701008_1.html))
- **NISM certification remains mandatory**, but the base exam no longer needs re-taking
- **Research Analyst** — research-report-based recommendations any investor may act on at their own discretion
- **Investment Adviser** — personalised advice after assessing income, risk appetite, goals
- **Trading calls have been excluded from the definition of "investment advice"**

> **What this means here:** with FRM Level-1 and NSE Technical Analyst certification, RA registration is realistically a NISM exam away. That flips it from barrier to **moat** — a SEBI-registered analyst teaching AI-assisted research is a category almost no competitor will bother entering.

**This is a reading of regulatory summaries by a non-lawyer. Confirm both the registration path and the trading-calls exclusion with a securities lawyer before relying on either.** The conservative operating boundary in `../00-strategy/risks-and-constraints.md` stands until that confirmation exists.

## What the research did *not* support

Honesty about what failed to validate matters as much as what did:

- **No evidence** that selling "AI agent packs" as a standalone low-ticket product is a proven category at scale. It remains a reasonable bet built on existing assets, not a validated model. Treat Phase 4's first form as an experiment.
- **No evidence** that a US-first market order beats a home-market-first order for an operator with no US logos. The credibility bridge is a hypothesis, not a finding.
- The creator-economy revenue figures come from sources with an incentive to inflate. Discounted accordingly throughout.
