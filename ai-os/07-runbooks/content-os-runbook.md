# Content OS — The Runbook

**What this is:** the repeatable loop that turns an idea into a published, measured video and eight platform assets. Not the strategy — the *procedure*. Follow it in order.

**The rule underneath everything:** every step reads Brand Brain first. One source of voice, price and proof means drift has one place to be corrected instead of nine.

---

## The loop

```
  RESEARCH ──▶ SCRIPT ──▶ PACKAGE ──▶ FILM ──▶ BRIEF ──▶ [EDIT] ──▶ SHORTS
     Sat        Sat/Sun     Sun        Thu       Thu       48h        post
                                                                        │
     ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
     ▼
  GATE ──▶ PUBLISH ──▶ REPURPOSE ──▶ MEASURE ──▶ back to RESEARCH
  pre-pub   Tue/Fri      T+24h          Mon
```

Eleven steps. Six are a skill you invoke, three are human work, two are gates.

---

## Where each step lands in the week

| Day | Step | Owner | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | 11 · Measure | Operator | 1 |
| **Tue** | 9 · Publish + 10 · Repurpose starts | Operator | 2 |
| **Wed** | (live session) | Paras | 1.5 |
| **Thu** | 4 · Film · 5 · Brief | Paras + Editor | 3 |
| **Fri** | 9 · Publish | Operator | 2 |
| **Sat** | 1 · Research · 2 · Script | Paras | 3 |
| **Sun** | 3 · Package · next week locked | Paras + Operator | 1.5 |

**Two videos a week means the loop runs twice, offset by three days.** Tuesday's video is in EDIT while Friday's is in SCRIPT. Keep a three-week buffer of filmed material — when it drops below two, Thursday's filming overrides everything.

---

## Step 1 · RESEARCH

**Owner** Paras · **When** Saturday · **Time** 45 min per topic

**Do this:**

```
/paras-research
```
Then paste: the rough topic idea, and which vertical it serves (real estate / finance / travel / consulting / education).

**You get back:** saturation analysis across four creator groups, the named content gap, three ranked angles, cross-links to existing videos, keywords, and a Competition-vs-Opportunity score.

**The gate:** net score **> 7.5 = make it · 6–7.5 = refine · < 6 = pass.** Do not argue with a sub-6 score because you like the topic. That is exactly the bias the score exists to catch.

**Hand off:** the chosen angle + vertical + emotional trigger → Step 2.

**Fails when:** you give it a topic instead of a problem. "AI for real estate" gets a vague answer. "Real estate agents losing leads because nobody answers WhatsApp at 9pm" gets a usable one.

---

## Step 2 · SCRIPT

**Owner** Paras · **When** Saturday, straight after research · **Time** 60–90 min

**Do this:**

```
/exp-brand-brain brief C3
```
Copy what it returns — voice, frameworks, open loops, never-say. Then:

```
/paras-script-writer
```
Paste: the Brand Brain slice, the chosen angle, the vertical, the target length, and whether this is a Meera continuation.

**You get back:** three hook options to choose from, then the full script with timing marks (0:30 open, 2:00 subscribe, beats every 90s, voice-drop at 6:00, close 9:15–10:00), a monetisation layer table, and five suggested Shorts timestamps.

**The gate:** read it aloud. If a sentence doesn't sound like something you'd say on a call, cut it. **3E check** — does it carry Emotion, Entertainment *and* Education? Two out of three is a weak video.

**Hand off:** final script → Steps 3 and 5. The five Shorts timestamps → Step 7.

**Fails when:** you accept the first hook. Pick from all three, out loud.

---

## Step 3 · PACKAGE

**Owner** Paras · **When** Sunday · **Time** 30 min

```
/paras-titles-thumbnails
```
Paste: the final script, the vertical, the emotional trigger, and the titles of your last 4 videos.

**You get back:** title options checked for mobile truncation, thumbnail briefs, and the emotional trigger for each.

**The gate:** the last-4-titles input matters — **the same emotional trigger three videos running is topic fatigue.** If the agent proposes a trigger you just used twice, ask for a different one.

**Hand off:** chosen title + thumbnail brief → Step 5 (brief) and Step 9 (publish). If the thumbnail pulls a frame from footage, that instruction goes in the edit brief.

---

## Step 4 · FILM

**Owner** Paras · **When** Thursday · **Time** 2–3 hrs for a batch

Batch-film. Never film on a publish day.

**Produce, without exception, a footage list:** every take, with timecodes, and a one-line note on what it covers. Step 5 cannot work without it, and briefing cuts that don't exist is the fastest way to lose a day.

**Hand off:** raw files + footage list → Step 5.

---

## Step 5 · EDITOR BRIEF

**Owner** Paras · **When** Thursday, same session as filming · **Time** 30 min per video

```
/exp-editor-brief
```
Paste: the final script, the footage list with timecodes, the chosen title and thumbnail direction.

**You get back:** hook specified to the frame, shot list with in/out timecodes, b-roll callouts, exact overlay copy, screen-recording specs, music direction, pacing notes, thumbnail frame, do-not-cut moments, and Shorts candidates.

**The gate — the only one that counts:** send it, then ask the editor *"could you work this without asking me anything?"* Every question they ask is a gap in the template. **Fix the template, not the instance** — the same gap will recur next week.

**Hand off:** brief + footage → the editor. SLA: **48 hours** long-form, 24 hours shorts.

---

## Step 6 · EDIT

**Owner** Editor · **Time** 48 hrs

The editor should be deciding *nothing*. Not the hook, not the title, not what the script meant. If they are, Step 5 was underspecified.

**Hand off:** finished file → Step 7 and Step 8.

---

## Step 7 · SHORTS

**Owner** Editor, spec'd by agent · **When** after the edit lands

```
/paras-shorts-selector
```
Paste: the script, the five suggested timestamps from Step 2, and the finished video length.

**You get back:** 3–5 cuts with in/out points, why each stands alone, and a rewritten hook for each — a short needs its own hook, not the long-form's.

**Publish:** Wednesday and Sunday evenings, Indian prime time.

---

## Step 8 · THE GATE

**Owner** Operator · **When** before every publish · **Time** 15 min

```
/paras-compliance-audit
```
Paste: the full package — script, title, thumbnail brief, description, tags, pinned comment, Skool post, Community Tab post, GHL email, Shorts selections.

**You get back:** a line-by-line compliance report against all 11 playbooks, every gap flagged, with specific fixes.

**This is a gate, not a review.** Publish takes its output as a required input. Nothing ships red.

**Catches:** stale prices, unevidenced claims, promises made on camera that become open loops, missing disclaimers, off-voice lines.

**Anything promised on camera goes into Brand Brain's open-loops section the same day.** A promise you forget is a debt the audience remembers.

---

## Step 9 · PUBLISH

**Owner** Operator · **When** Tuesday and Friday, 9am IST sharp · **Time** 2 hrs across 48h

```
/paras-publishing-package
```
Paste: the final video, transcript, chosen title, thumbnail, and current offers from Brand Brain.

**You get back:** SEO description, chapters, tags, pinned comment, Skool post, Community Tab post, GHL email, and a promise-checker scan of the transcript.

**Then run the 48-hour protocol:**

| T+ | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Publish 9am. Pinned comment live, with the current CTA link. | O |
| 0–2h | **Reply to every comment.** Cannot be delegated to a schedule. | Paras |
| +2h | Community Tab post | O |
| +3h | IG story teaser | O |
| +4h | Skool post | O |
| +6h | Check CTR/AVD; flag if off-target | O |
| +24h | GHL email · IG carousel | O |
| +48h | LinkedIn long-form · WhatsApp broadcast | O |

**UTMs on every link, no exceptions.** Step 11 is blind without them.

---

## Step 10 · REPURPOSE

**Owner** Operator · **When** T+24h · **Time** 1 hr

```
/paras-repurpose
```
Paste: the video transcript, the title, and both voice registers from Brand Brain.

**You get back:** LinkedIn long-form (English, client audience), IG carousel (Hinglish hooks), X thread, Facebook post, WhatsApp broadcast, GMB post.

**Hard rule: never the same text across platforms.** Native or nothing. This is one task, not six.

**The Skool drop carries the actual working agent**, not a description of it — that is what makes the membership justify itself weekly without a pitch.

**This is the first step Paras hands over entirely.** If he is still writing LinkedIn posts in week three, the lane is not working.

---

## Step 11 · MEASURE

**Owner** Operator, read by Paras · **When** Monday · **Time** 1 hr

```
/paras-ctr-audit
```
Paste: the last 4 weeks of analytics — CTR, AVD, views, subs — or a YouTube Studio export.

**You get back:** each video against the **4% / 6% / 8% targets at day 30 / 60 / 90**, the specific failure for each underperformer (title, thumbnail, mobile truncation, trigger mismatch, topic fatigue), three A/B swap options each, and channel-wide systemic flags.

**Act on it the same morning.** A swap recommendation you read and don't execute is worse than not running the audit — it costs the time and produces nothing.

**Feed forward:** the systemic flags go into next Saturday's Research step. That is what closes the loop and makes week 6 better than week 1.

---

## Weekly review — 5 questions, Monday, 15 min

1. Did we publish twice?
2. What did the CTR audit say, and what one thing does it say to fix?
3. What did Paras get pulled into that wasn't camera, offers, calls or judgment?
4. Which agent produced output that needed fixing — Brand Brain gap, or agent gap?
5. What did we do three times by hand that should become an agent?

Q3 is the health check. Q5 is where the roster grows.

---

## What breaks this loop

| Symptom | Real cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Editor keeps asking questions | Brief was abstract | Fix the template, not the answer |
| Publishing slips | Pipeline under 3 weeks | Thursday filming overrides everything |
| Output sounds generic | Agents not reading Brand Brain | Run `/exp-brand-brain brief` first, every time |
| CTR flat for weeks | Same emotional trigger repeating | Feed the last 4 titles into Step 3 |
| Paras still doing operator work | Lane unclear | Write the missing decision into Brand Brain once |
| Repurposing feels like six jobs | Being run as six tasks | One task, one agent, operator-owned |
