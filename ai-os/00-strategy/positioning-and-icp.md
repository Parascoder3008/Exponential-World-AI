# Positioning and ICP

## The position

> **A business person teaching AI, not a tech person teaching business.**

Hold this line in every asset. It is the whole differentiator, and it decays the moment content drifts technical. "How transformers work" is off-position. "The three hours a day your ops coordinator spends on work a system should do" is on-position.

Two audiences, two languages, one position:

| | Creator / channel audience | Business / client audience |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Indian SMB owners, Tier 2/3, plus creators and coaches | SMB and mid-market operators, US → UAE → India |
| Language | Hinglish | English |
| Channel | YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, Skool, WhatsApp | Outbound, LinkedIn, workshops, referral |
| They buy | Workshop, bootcamp, cohort, agent packs | Audit, install, consulting, DFY |
| Proof that moves them | Watching him build it live | Nippon, Pluto Travels, Wingsway |

Same system, two doors. The channel is not lead-gen *for* the agency — it is a business in its own right that happens to also produce the agency's best leads.

## The client ICP

Market order: **United States → UAE → India.** This is Paras's call and it is the harder path — he has no US logos, which is why the credibility bridge in `paras-lead-engine/references/outreach.md` exists. Do not quietly re-target to India because it converts easier; if a US niche genuinely will not work, say why.

### Tier 1 — SMB, 5–50 staff (start here)

Owner-operated. The founder decides and is reachable. Cycle 1–3 weeks, nobody to get past.

Med spas, dental and specialty clinics, law firms, real estate teams, home services, boutique agencies, training institutes, travel operators, fitness studios, accounting practices.

Buying signal: **already spending** — on ads, on a hire, on tooling. An SMB with no spend anywhere is not a prospect, it is a hobby.

### Tier 2 — Mid-market, 50–500 staff

A marketing or ops lead exists; budget is a line item. Cycle 3–8 weeks.

Multi-location healthcare, regional developers, mid-size financial advisory, education groups, logistics, hospitality groups, B2B services.

Buying signal: a department hiring coordination roles while the founder talks publicly about growth. Expansion plus manual process equals urgency.

### Tier 3 — Enterprise, 500+

Where the existing proof lives. **These do not come from cold email.** They come from workshops, referrals, speaking, and inbound. For this tier the job is intelligence briefs and warm-path maps, not sequences — and the Wednesday workshop is where enterprise buyers self-qualify.

### The four qualifiers

In-ICP requires **all four**: verified operating (fetched this session) · money is moving · a reachable human decides · a real 4C gap scoring 35+ with evidence. Fail one and it is not a lead yet — note which one, because some become leads later.

### Hard disqualifiers

In-house AI team · another AI agency's badge on the site · under 5 staff with no spend · dead or parked domain · regulated beyond current posture (US healthcare with PHI, US financial advice licensing) · already automated, 4C under 15 · asked not to be contacted (permanent, all lists, forever).

## The enterprise question

Paras has asked whether this works at enterprise level. It does, but it is a different motion and it should not be chased in year one.

What changes: procurement, security review, and a 3–9 month cycle replace a founder saying yes on a call. What does not change: the 4C diagnostic, which reads a 5,000-person company exactly as well as a 15-person one — the gaps are just bigger and cost more.

The realistic enterprise path is the one that already produced Nippon and Wingsway: **a paid training programme is the wedge.** Train the team, find the workflows, propose the build. Enterprise buys training from an outsider far more readily than it buys automation from one, because training is low-risk and creates the internal champion who later sponsors the build.

So enterprise stays in the plan as an *inbound and referral* motion fed by workshops and the channel, and gets a named-account intelligence effort rather than a cold sequence. It is not a Phase 1 or Phase 2 target.
