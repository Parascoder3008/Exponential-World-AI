## The three numbers that decide whether this worked

At week 13, everything else is commentary:

1. **26 publishes.** Two a week, thirteen weeks, no exceptions. This is the one that fails first and quietest.
2. **One hands-off week.** The loop ran without Paras in it.
3. **First install sold and delivered**, with a case study captured.

Miss the third and you are early. Miss the second and you built tools, not a system. **Miss the first and none of the rest matters** — the channel is the bottleneck, and a quarter of build progress with a broken publishing rhythm is a quarter spent going backwards.

## Weekly review, every Monday, 30 minutes

Five questions. Same five, every week.

1. Did we publish twice last week?
2. What did the scorecard say, and what single thing does it say to fix?
3. What did Paras get pulled into that wasn't camera, offers, calls, or judgment?
4. Which agent produced output that needed fixing, and is that a Brand Brain gap or an agent gap?
5. What did we do three times by hand that should become an agent?

Question 3 is the health check on the whole system. Question 5 is where the roster grows — new agents come from observed repetition, not from this document.
