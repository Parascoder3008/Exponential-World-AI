## The four numbers that decide whether this worked

At week 13, everything else is commentary:

1. **Revenue up.** Sprint 0 plus Phase 1 sales should have moved the monthly number materially off ₹1–5L. This is the one v1 didn't measure and should have.
2. **26 publishes.** Two a week, thirteen weeks. Fails first and quietest.
3. **One hands-off week.** The loop ran without Paras in it.
4. **First install sold and delivered**, with a case study captured.

Miss #4 and you're early. Miss #3 and you built tools, not a system. Miss #2 and the asset you're harvesting is decaying. **Miss #1 and none of the rest matters** — at 3–6 months of runway, a quarter that produces systems but not revenue is a quarter that shortens the runway by a quarter.

## Weekly review, every Monday, 30 minutes

Six questions. Same six, every week.

1. **What did we sell last week?**
2. Did we publish twice?
3. What did the scorecard say, and what single thing does it say to fix?
4. What did Paras get pulled into that wasn't camera, offers, calls, or judgment?
5. Which agent produced output that needed fixing — Brand Brain gap or agent gap?
6. What did we do three times by hand that should become an agent?

Question 1 is new in v2 and goes first deliberately. Question 4 is the health check on the system. Question 6 is where the roster grows — new agents come from observed repetition, not from this document.
