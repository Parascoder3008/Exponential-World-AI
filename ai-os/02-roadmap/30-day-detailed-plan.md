## The webinar decision

The trading webinar converts cold, but the YouTube audience is SMB-AI. Running a trading pitch at them is a mismatch, and building nothing wastes the idle asset.

So: **build the SMB AI webinar on the proven 8-block architecture.** Same structure that already works — cold-open contrast hook, authority stack, three promises, framework, five-tool walkthrough with a poll after each, live demo, the turn, offer. Different content. `paras-webinar-builder` produces exactly this.

**Working title:** *AI Business OS — How Indian SMBs Run Marketing, Sales and Ops With AI Agents*

**Two CTAs, because benchmarks show two outperforms one:**

| CTA | Offer | For |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | The cohort, ₹25–50K | Creators, solopreneurs, small teams |
| **2** | Free 4C Audit | Business owners — routes to Workshop and Install |

One webinar, both revenue paths. CTA 2 is how a ₹0 attendee becomes a ₹1.5L workshop conversation.

**The trading webinar still runs** — as a low-effort encore to the trading list only on **Wed 23 Sep**. Existing deck, no new build, pure harvest. It never touches the YouTube channel.

Paras's realistic weekday: **~6 focused hours.** The block structure matters more than the clock.

| Block | Time | What |

## What breaks this

**Content eats the outreach hour.** Block 1 before Block 2, every day. Content is the enjoyable work and it will win if allowed.

**The webinar slips.** Wed 16 Sep is fixed. A deck that's 80% ready on the day beats a perfect deck in October.

**Follow-up stops at three touches.** Most people quit there. Most revenue is past it.

**Publishing pauses for the launch.** The channel is the asset being harvested — damaging it to harvest it defeats the point.

**Everything becomes must-do.** Two must-dos a day, maximum. A list where everything is critical is a list where nothing is.
