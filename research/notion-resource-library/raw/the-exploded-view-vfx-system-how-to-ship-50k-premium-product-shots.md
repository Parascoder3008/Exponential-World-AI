## ⚙️ Step 1 — The Base-Clip Capture Protocol
The exploded-view effect requires a specific base clip. Get the capture wrong and no VFX will save the shot.
### The 5 Capture Rules
1. **Static shot only.** No camera movement. The camera holds still. Your body holds still. Only your hands and product move.
2. **Product centered in frame + at chest height.** Not at head height. Not below waist. Chest height gives the "hero product" positioning.
3. **Even natural lighting from front.** Not overhead. Not backlit. Softbox or window light from directly in front (or 3/4 front). This makes relighting the exploded components easier.
4. **Plain background (soft neutral wall or subtle texture).** No cluttered background. No competing objects behind the product.
5. **5-8 second duration.** Under 5 = not enough time for the VFX arc. Over 8 = viewers scroll.
### The Ideal Base Clip Shot List
**Beat 1 (0-1.5s)**: You hold product centered in frame, look at camera briefly, then shift eyes to product.
**Beat 2 (1.5-3s)**: You bring product slightly closer to camera, subtle gesture of "watch this."
**Beat 3 (3-5s)**: You hold product still, gaze at product with subtle expression of pride/curiosity.
**Beat 4 (5-6.5s)**: Slight micro-gesture of your hand releasing product's grip (as if it will disassemble).
**Beat 5 (6.5-8s)**: You return to holding product firmly, look up at camera with subtle smile.
The VFX will fit disassembly into Beat 3-4 and reassembly at Beat 5. Your job in the base clip is to leave that window open.
### Camera Setup
→ Camera: iPhone 15 Pro+ / Android flagship / Sony ZV series / any 4K<br>→ Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical (native mobile deploy) OR 16:9 horizontal (YouTube / brand site)<br>→ Frame rate: 30fps<br>→ Resolution: 4K minimum (sub-4K = drift in VFX rendering)<br>→ Distance from camera: 3-4 feet (product visible at chest height with room around)
### Lighting Setup
**Option A (Studio)**: 1 softbox 3/4 front of you, 1 fill light from opposite side at half intensity, subtle rim light behind. Consistent daylight balance (5600K).
**Option B (Natural)**: Sit facing a large window with soft indirect daylight. Overcast day is ideal. Add a bounce card (white foam or reflector) on opposite side for fill.
**Option C (Minimum)**: Any bright, evenly-lit space. Avoid harsh shadows. Test by holding your hand next to product — if shadow is soft and even, lighting is fine.

## ⚙️ Step 3 — The Gemini Omni Flash Exploded-View Prompt Template
The prompt that tells Gemini Omni Flash exactly what to do.
### The Template
```javascript
Exploded-view VFX transformation. Original clip:
subject holding [PRODUCT NAME] at chest height
in [ENVIRONMENT DESCRIPTION].

Preserve exactly:
→ Subject face + identity
→ Subject gestures + timing
→ Camera position + trajectory (static)
→ Background environment
→ Overall shot duration ([SECONDS]s)

Transform beginning at [START TIME]s:
The product held by subject begins to disassemble
in mid-air. Individual components separate with
realistic physics — each component has appropriate
weight, momentum, and trajectory.

Components to expose (referenced from Cinema
Studio):
→ @[COMPONENT_1]: [POSITION relative to product]
→ @[COMPONENT_2]: [POSITION]
→ @[COMPONENT_3]: [POSITION]
→ @[COMPONENT_4]: [POSITION]

Physics rules:
→ Heavier components float lower + slower
→ Lighter components drift higher + faster
→ Consistent gravity vector (down)
→ Slight rotation on components as they separate
→ No collisions — clean separation

At [MID TIME]s:
Peak of the exploded view. All components
visible around subject. Subject's expression:
subtle pride, curiosity, or wonder.

Beginning at [REASSEMBLE TIME]s:
Components reverse their trajectory and
reassemble into the original product form.
Motion should feel intentional, not chaotic.
Subject's hand closes back around the reassembled
product.

At [END TIME]s:
Product is fully reassembled in subject's hand.
Subject looks back at camera with subtle smile.

Lighting adaptation:
Each floating component receives lighting from
the same light source as the original scene
(top-left soft). Cast realistic subtle shadows
on the background where appropriate.

Additional constraints:
→ 4K output
→ Match original clip's color grade
→ No motion blur on the disassembly (crisp
  components)
→ Subject relit slightly to account for
  components in front of face
```
### Fill Variables Per Shot
→ **PRODUCT NAME**: your actual product<br>→ **ENVIRONMENT DESCRIPTION**: match base clip environment<br>→ **START TIME**: typically 2-3 seconds in<br>→ **MID TIME**: typically 4-5 seconds in<br>→ **REASSEMBLE TIME**: typically 5.5-6.5 seconds in<br>→ **END TIME**: match original clip duration<br>→ **COMPONENT_1-4**: reference Cinema Studio Element names
### Running the Render
1. Open Higgsfield → select Gemini Omni Flash model
2. Attach your base clip as reference video
3. Paste the filled Exploded-View prompt
4. Verify component assets are linked from Cinema Studio
5. Set output: 4K, match original aspect ratio, match original duration
6. Render
Render time: 3-6 minutes per shot.
Cost: $4-$8 per render depending on complexity + duration.

## ⚙️ Step 4 — The CapCut Brand Overlay Protocol
Gemini Omni Flash renders the VFX. CapCut adds the brand information layer that turns it into an ad.
### 5-Minute Overlay Protocol
**Minute 0-1**: Import<br>→ Import Gemini Omni Flash output MP4 into CapCut<br>→ Verify aspect ratio matches deploy target
**Minutes 1-2**: Component Callout Labels<br>→ At the "peak exploded view" moment (typically 4.5-5s), add text callouts pointing to each floating component<br>→ Font: Bold sans-serif (Inter Bold, TikCut Sans, or platform-native)<br>→ Style: White text, black stroke, small size (readable but not dominant)<br>→ Position: Small labels beside each floating component<br>→ Animation: Fade in over 0.5s at peak moment, hold, fade out during reassembly
**Minutes 2-3**: Brand Elements<br>→ Brand logo bottom-right of final frame (28-30s if 30s ad)<br>→ Fade in during reassembly (5-5.5s)<br>→ Hold through final smile
**Minutes 3-4**: CTA + Product Name<br>→ Product name overlay at bottom during final beat<br>→ CTA text at end frame: "SHOP", "GET IT", "IN BIO", etc.<br>→ Match brand typography rules
**Minute 4-5**: Export<br>→ Export at 4K resolution<br>→ 30fps<br>→ File naming: `[BRAND]_EXPLODED_[PRODUCT]_v1.mp4`
Total polish time: 5 minutes per ad.

## ⚙️ What NOT to Do
→ Don't skip the base-clip capture protocol. Bad framing / lighting / duration = no VFX will save the shot.
→ Don't shoot the base clip with camera movement. Static shot only. Camera movement forces Gemini Omni Flash to guess at trajectory + destabilizes the exploded components.
→ Don't skip GPT Image 2 component asset generation. Without reference assets, Gemini Omni Flash guesses at what your product's internals look like. Result: generic components that don't tell your product's specific story.
→ Don't reuse a component asset for one product on a different product. Each product needs its own component asset library.
→ Don't over-populate the exploded view. 4-5 components is optimal. 8+ components looks cluttered and viewer can't focus.
→ Don't skip the reassembly beat. An exploded view without reassembly feels unresolved. Every exploded-view ad needs the "everything comes back together" beat.
→ Don't skip the component labels in CapCut. The labels are what turn the visual into a marketing message. Without them, viewers see a cool effect but don't retain what makes your product special.
→ Don't use dramatic music. This format needs subtle music or apartment ambience. Overpowering music competes with the visual reveal.
→ Don't measure on CTR alone. Exploded-view ads win on saves + shares + hold rate + brand recall. If you evaluate on CTR, you'll kill winners.
→ Don't run exploded-view as your only ad format. It's premium-positioning content. Pair with reaction UGC + standard product ads to cover full funnel.
→ Don't skip 4K throughout the chain. Sub-4K anywhere = drift in the VFX. Modern iPhones capture 4K natively.
→ Don't try to VFX a product that has no interesting internal story. Some products don't lend themselves to exploded-view (single-material solid objects with no components). Match the format to the product.
