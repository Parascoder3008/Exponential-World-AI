## ⚙️ Step 2 — The Mascot Illustration Integration Protocol
The mascot illustration is what gives the cozy lifestyle storyboard its family-brand signature. Here's how to integrate it properly.
### Mascot Selection
**Option A — Use existing brand mascot**:<br>If your brand already has a mascot (Tony the Tiger, Cadbury bunny, coffee bean character, etc.), reference it directly in the prompt: "Cute [BRAND MASCOT NAME] illustration in top-right, matching brand's established mascot style."
**Option B — Category-appropriate mascot**:<br>If no brand mascot exists, use a category-appropriate one:<br>→ Chocolate: chocolate square mascot with smile<br>→ Coffee: coffee bean character with steam<br>→ Cereal: cereal grain character with bowl<br>→ Kids' snacks: cute animal (bunny, bear, mouse)<br>→ Tea / cocoa: steaming mug character with face
**Option C — Custom brand mascot generation**:<br>Generate a custom mascot in GPT Image 2 first: "Design a cute friendly [PRODUCT CATEGORY] mascot in Pentagon-style illustration, warm colors, kid-friendly, brand-safe."<br>Save as `@brand_mascot` for reuse across storyboards.
### Mascot Placement Rules
→ **Top-right of header**: always (Behance signature placement)<br>→ **Small appearances throughout grid**: 3-5 panels, appearing "naturally" near product moments (not in every panel)<br>→ **Final hero shot (Scene 12)**: mascot beside product pack
### Mascot Style Anchors
Ensure your mascot has:<br>→ Rounded soft edges (never sharp/angular)<br>→ Warm color palette matching brand<br>→ Simple flat illustration style (Pentagon / Muji aesthetic)<br>→ Friendly expression (not cartoonish/childish for teen+ audience)<br>→ Consistent scale across all appearances
### Anti-Anchors for Mascot
→ Never photorealistic (breaks the cozy illustration feel)<br>→ Never in the hero photo shots (only in graphic overlay space)<br>→ Never dominating the panel (5-10% of card area max)<br>→ Never in every panel (feels cluttered)

## ⚙️ Step 3 — The Seedance 2.0 Storyboard-to-Video Conversion Protocol
Once the storyboard is complete, convert the same 12-scene structure into an actual 30-second video.
### The Seedance Master Video Prompt
```javascript
DTC COZY LIFESTYLE POV HAND COMMERCIAL —
SEEDANCE 2.0

Duration: 30 seconds
Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical
Style: POV hands only (no face visible), cozy
kitchen environment, warm golden morning
sunlight, soft natural shadows, creamy neutral
color palette, realistic product photography,
cinematic composition, shallow depth of field,
premium commercial lighting, lifestyle
advertising aesthetic.

Character: Hands only. Consistent hand identity
across all 12 beats. Same skin tone, same
manicure state, warm relatable feel.

Product: [PRODUCT NAME + DETAILED APPEARANCE]

Audio: Native audio + warm feel-good music
- Warm happy instrumental soundtrack (subtle,
  no lyrics or minimal vocals)
- Ambient cozy kitchen sounds (kettle, spoon
  clink, gentle stirring)
- Subtle SFX per beat: packet tear, pour,
  stir, sip
- Warm feel-good mood throughout

12-Beat Sequence (each 2.5 seconds):

[PASTE 12-SCENE STRUCTURE FROM STORYBOARD]

Continuity locks:
→ Same hands, same warm kitchen counter, same
  golden lighting across all beats
→ Product identity locked across every beat
→ Camera POV: consistent close-up hands
  perspective
→ Aesthetic: cozy family-brand warm throughout

Output: 30-second vertical POV hands cozy
lifestyle commercial with warm feel-good audio
```
### Rendering Approach
**Option A — Single-Render Mega-Prompt (Recommended)**:
1. Feed the entire 12-beat sequence as one 30-second Seedance render
2. Attach product photo reference
3. Attach hand reference (or let Seedance generate consistent hands)
4. Set output: 9:16, 30 seconds, 4K
Render time: 6-10 minutes.<br>Cost: $6-$10 per full video.
**Option B — Multi-Beat Render + CapCut Assembly**:
1. Render each 2.5-second beat separately
2. Assemble in CapCut with soft crossfade transitions (warm cozy feel)
3. Layer additional audio if needed
Total render time: 20-30 minutes across 12 renders.<br>Cost: $3-$5 total.

## ⚙️ Step 5 — The CapCut Assembly Protocol
For final polish. 5 minutes.
### 5-Minute Assembly
**Minutes 0-1**: Import<br>→ Import Seedance MP4 (or 12 separate beats)<br>→ Verify 9:16 aspect + 30-second total duration
**Minute 1-2**: Doodle + Mascot Overlays<br>→ Add hand-drawn hearts + sparkles at warm moments<br>→ Add subtle mascot appearance at end scenes<br>→ Font for any handwritten notes: Marker / Sacramento
**Minute 2-3**: Music + Audio Balance<br>→ Add warm feel-good instrumental (subtle, no dominant vocals)<br>→ Volume: -12 to -8 dB<br>→ Kitchen ambient sounds layered underneath
**Minute 3-4**: Brand Overlay<br>→ Add brand logo top-right (small, subtle)<br>→ Product name overlay at final beat<br>→ Brand tagline if applicable
**Minute 4-5**: Export<br>→ 4K vertical, 30fps<br>→ File naming: `[BRAND]_COZY-COMMERCIAL_[PRODUCT]_v1.mp4`
Total polish time: 5 minutes.

## ⚙️ What NOT to Do
→ Don't skip the storyboard step. The storyboard is share-worthy content that multiplies output.
→ Don't show a face in POV Hand Cozy Commercial. POV hands only.
→ Don't add loud vocal music. Warm feel-good instrumental only.
→ Don't compress under 12 beats. Fewer than 12 = missed cozy moments.
→ Don't over-populate mascot appearances. 3-5 panels max, not every panel.
→ Don't measure cozy commercials on CTR alone. Measure hold rate + saves + brand recall.
→ Don't ignore the algorithm signal. Cozy content compounds algorithmic reach.
→ Don't deploy at low audio quality. Warm ambient audio is critical.
→ Don't skip captions on deploy platforms. Silent-viewing viewers need caption fallback.
→ Don't use dialogue-heavy scripts. Brief voice-over OK. Long monologues break cozy feel.
→ Don't skip the storyboard deployment. Post on LinkedIn/Twitter as content. Free brand marketing.
→ Don't reuse identical mascot across all products. Match mascot to product category.
→ Don't force a mascot if brand doesn't have one. Use category-appropriate friendly character.
