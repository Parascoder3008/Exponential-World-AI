## ⚙️ Component 1 — The DTC Creative Flywheel Map
The full picture of how creative moves from idea to deployed ad. Every component of the system feeds into this flywheel.
### The 6-Stage Flywheel
```plain text
[1. IDEAS] → [2. HOOKS] → [3. CONCEPTS] → [4. PRODUCTION] → [5. DEPLOYMENT] → [6. REPORTING]
       ↑                                                                              ↓
       └──────────────────── FEEDBACK LOOP ───────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
### Stage 1 — IDEAS
**Input**: Trend signals + competitor scans + customer language + product context<br>**Output**: Priority idea list for the week<br>**Skill used**: Concept Generator + Trend Radar + Competitor Audit<br>**Time**: 30 minutes Monday morning
### Stage 2 — HOOKS
**Input**: Priority ideas from Stage 1<br>**Output**: 20-30 scored hooks across psychological drivers<br>**Skill used**: Hook Lab + Hook Templates library<br>**Time**: 30 minutes Monday morning
### Stage 3 — CONCEPTS
**Input**: Approved hooks from Stage 2<br>**Output**: 5-7 full ad concepts with format + angle + funnel position<br>**Skill used**: Concept Generator + Storyboard Sketcher<br>**Time**: 60 minutes Monday morning
### Stage 4 — PRODUCTION
**Input**: Approved concepts from Stage 3<br>**Output**: 5-7 finished ads (Seedance renders + CapCut polish)<br>**Skill used**: Production Brief Templates + Seedance rendering<br>**Time**: 2-3 hours Tuesday
### Stage 5 — DEPLOYMENT
**Input**: Finished ads from Stage 4<br>**Output**: Live ads on Meta + TikTok + YouTube<br>**Skill used**: Multi-platform aspect adapter + Meta Ads Manager<br>**Time**: 30 minutes Tuesday afternoon
### Stage 6 — REPORTING
**Input**: Live ad performance data (24-72 hours)<br>**Output**: Kill/scale decisions + weekly executive summary<br>**Skill used**: Hour-24 Matrix + Weekly Dashboard<br>**Time**: 30 minutes Wednesday + 30 minutes Friday
### The Feedback Loop
Reporting from Stage 6 feeds back to Stage 1:<br>→ Winning hook patterns → refine Hook Templates library<br>→ Winning format patterns → prioritize in next week's concepts<br>→ Kill list patterns → anti-patterns for next week's ideation<br>→ Fatigue signals → trigger variant production before burnout
The flywheel compounds every week. Winners get amplified, losers get filtered out at the ideation stage.

## ⚙️ Component 2 — The Weekly Production Workflow
The repeatable process that keeps output consistent.
### Monday — Concept Sprint (2 hours)
**9:00-9:30**: Run trend scans + competitor audit + budget leak audit<br>**9:30-10:00**: Generate 20 ad concepts + 30 hooks<br>**10:00-10:30**: Founder review + approve top 5 concepts<br>**10:30-11:00**: Generate production briefs per approved concept
### Tuesday — Production Sprint (3 hours)
**9:00-10:00**: Generate character sheets + product sheets + location sheets (if needed)<br>**10:00-12:00**: Render 5-7 ads in Seedance 2.0/2.5<br>**12:00-13:00**: CapCut polish per ad (brand overlay + music + captions)<br>**13:00-13:30**: Deploy to Meta Ads Manager + TikTok Ads Manager
### Wednesday — Matrix Sprint (1 hour)
**9:00-9:30**: Run hour-24 kill/scale matrix on Tuesday batch<br>**9:30-10:00**: Execute matrix decisions + queue winner variants
### Thursday — Diagnostic Sprint (as needed)
Run diagnostics on any underperforming ad:<br>→ Hook rate under 30%: run Hook Rate Diagnostician<br>→ Hold rate collapse: run Hold Rate Auditor<br>→ CPA spike: run Budget Leak Auditor
### Friday — Review Sprint (1 hour)
**9:00-9:30**: Run fatigue detector on all active ads<br>**9:30-10:00**: Compile weekly kill list + failure patterns<br>**10:00-10:30**: Generate weekly executive report + send to founder
### Weekly Time Investment
**Total operator time**: 7-8 hours across 5 days<br>**Equivalent agency work**: 40-60 hours<br>**Operator can manage**: 3-5 DTC brands simultaneously at this cadence

## ⚙️ Component 3 — Hook Templates (30 Openings Across 6 Psychological Drivers)
The opening lines that stop the scroll before the first second is up.
### Driver 1 — Curiosity Gap (5 hooks)
1. "Wait until you see what happens when [ACTION]."
2. "The [CATEGORY] founder nobody talks about."
3. "Why [COMMON BELIEF] is actually [OPPOSITE]."
4. "I tried [X] for [TIME]. Here's what changed."
5. "[NUMBER] things nobody tells you about [CATEGORY]."
### Driver 2 — Loss Aversion (5 hooks)
1. "You're losing $[X]/month if you're doing [MISTAKE]."
2. "Stop doing [ACTION]. Here's what works instead."
3. "The mistake costing DTC brands $10K/month."
4. "If you're [ACTION], you're leaving money on the table."
5. "Every day you wait, you lose [SPECIFIC OUTCOME]."
### Driver 3 — Identity Alignment (5 hooks)
1. "If you're [ICP], this is for you."
2. "The [PRODUCT] every serious [ROLE] uses."
3. "How top [CATEGORY] operators run their [WORKFLOW]."
4. "This is what [ASPIRATIONAL IDENTITY] looks like."
5. "You didn't build [BUSINESS] to [PAIN]. Here's the fix."
### Driver 4 — Social Proof Pattern-Interrupt (5 hooks)
1. "40+ DTC brands are quietly using this."
2. "Every DTC founder I know does this on Monday."
3. "The Claude skill that went viral for a reason."
4. "[NUMBER] operators saved $[X] this quarter using [X]."
5. "The playbook every 8-figure brand uses (nobody talks about)."
### Driver 5 — Contrarian Frame (5 hooks)
1. "Everyone tells you to [COMMON ADVICE]. It's wrong."
2. "Stop optimizing [X]. Optimize [Y] instead."
3. "The best DTC brands do the opposite of this."
4. "[CATEGORY GURU] is teaching this wrong."
5. "The DTC advice that's actually killing your ROAS."
### Driver 6 — Direct Benefit Contradiction (5 hooks)
1. "Ship 10x more ads without hiring anyone."
2. "$10K/month agency work for the cost of a coffee."
3. "AI ads that don't look like AI."
4. "Founder-quality creative without the founder time."
5. "Weekly ROAS reports without a media buyer."
Rotate across drivers. Track which drivers win per brand.

## ⚙️ Component 4 — The 4-Week Campaign Planner
Plan and execute Meta + TikTok campaigns without an agency holding your hand.
### The 4-Week Test → Validate → Scale → Refresh Loop
### Week 1 — Test (Concept Discovery)
**Goal**: Ship 5-10 new ad concepts across formats.<br>**Budget**: 30% of monthly ad budget for testing.<br>**Success criteria**: Identify 2-3 concepts that hit KILL/HOLD/SCALE thresholds.
**Deploy structure**:
- 2 UGC talking-head ads
- 2 Reaction UGC ads
- 2 Cinematic hero ads
- 2 BTS Vlog ads
- 2 Test-velocity 1-photo ads
**Decision point**: Hour-48 matrix identifies 2-3 winners to advance.
### Week 2 — Validate (Scale Winners)
**Goal**: 2-3 winners from Week 1 tested at 3x spend to confirm they hold.<br>**Budget**: 40% of monthly ad budget on Week 1 winners.<br>**Success criteria**: ROAS holds at 80%+ of test-phase performance at scale.
**Deploy structure**:
- 3x budget on each Week 1 winner
- New audience layer (lookalike expansion)
- New format variants for scalers
**Decision point**: Confirmed validators advance to Week 3 evergreen scale.
### Week 3 — Scale (Evergreen Deployment)
**Goal**: Push validated winners to max budget.<br>**Budget**: 60% of monthly ad budget on validated evergreens.<br>**Success criteria**: Sustained ROAS at target CPA.
**Deploy structure**:
- Full budget on 2-3 validated evergreens
- Continue testing 2-3 new concepts in parallel (Week 1 loop restarts)
**Decision point**: Watch for fatigue signals (frequency + CTR + CPA trending).
### Week 4 — Refresh (Fatigue Prevention)
**Goal**: Ship variants of scaling ads before fatigue.<br>**Budget**: 20% of monthly ad budget on fresh variants.<br>**Success criteria**: Variants pick up when originals show fatigue signals.
**Deploy structure**:
- 5 variants per scaling ad (hook / format / audience variations)
- Warm-up variants with $30-$50/day before promoting to full scale
- Kill original when variants outperform
**Decision point**: Rotate variants into evergreen slot as originals fatigue.
### Weekly Testing Volume
By end of Month 1: 25-40 total ads tested, 2-3 evergreens scaled, weekly refresh cadence locked.

## ⚙️ What NOT to Do
→ Don't skip the Flywheel Map. It's the mental model that ties the 6 components together.
→ Don't skip the Weekly Workflow schedule. Ad-hoc production doesn't compound.
→ Don't force operators to invent hooks. Give them the Hook Templates library.
→ Don't skip the Campaign Planner. Random ad tests without a 4-week loop = wasted budget.
→ Don't skip the Production Brief Templates. Free-form Claude prompts produce inconsistent output.
→ Don't skip the Weekly Dashboard. Without reporting, the flywheel breaks.
→ Don't cancel the agency in Week 1. Migrate in parallel for 3-4 weeks to validate.
→ Don't run this for accounts under $5K/mo spend. Variance is too high for pattern detection.
→ Don't skip founder review on Monday concept sprint. Operator + founder collaboration is what maintains brand quality.
→ Don't measure operators by "how much they produced." Measure by ROAS + fatigue prevention + winning concept identification.
