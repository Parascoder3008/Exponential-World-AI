## ⚙️ Step 1 — The Master Nano Banana Prompt
Paste this exact structure into Nano Banana (Google's Gemini Native Image tool). Fill the 2 variables per shot.
### The Master Prompt
```javascript
[subject], premium food advertising, white
seamless background, high key studio lighting,
floating stacked composition, levitating pieces,
[ingredient_bits] scattered, clean minimal
layout, crisp soft shadow on surface, ultra
realistic, macro product photography, 100mm lens
look, f/8 sharp focus, subtle texture detail,
editorial commercial, 8k
```
### The Variables
**[subject]** — your product. Be specific with material + shape + color:
- "a bottle of clear serum with gold cap"
- "3 chocolate-glazed donuts"
- "a stack of 5 nigiri sushi pieces"
- "a leather crossbody bag with brass hardware"
**[ingredient_bits]** — natural scatter elements that visually reinforce the product's story:
- Skincare: "oil droplets", "vitamin C molecules", "hyaluronic serum droplets"
- Supplements: "capsule powder", "granules", "compound crystals"
- Food/bev: "sesame seeds", "chocolate shavings", "ground coffee beans"
- Fashion: "gold thread", "leather flecks", "subtle fabric fibers"
- Cosmetics: "pigment particles", "shimmer specks", "powder dust"
- Tech: "metallic filaments", "component shards", "subtle particles"
### What Nano Banana Produces
A levitating stacked composition on a white seamless background with your subject in floating position, ingredient_bits scattered around it in mid-air, crisp soft shadow on the ground plane, editorial commercial aesthetic.
Generation time: 30-90 seconds per still.
Cost: \\~$2-$3 per generation.
### Prompt Variations for Different Compositions
Beyond the master prompt, you can vary composition style. Same base structure, different composition qualifier:
**Stacked vertical composition**: Replace "floating stacked composition, levitating pieces" with:
```javascript
tall vertical stacked composition, one piece
above the other with air gaps between, gravity-
defying stack
```
**Orbital composition**: Replace with:
```javascript
subject centered with orbital composition,
pieces suspended in circular pattern around
central hero, radial symmetry
```
**Cascade composition**: Replace with:
```javascript
cascading downward composition, pieces falling
in slow-motion arc, subject at top, ingredient
trail below
```
**Explosion composition**: Replace with:
```javascript
subject exploding outward composition, pieces
radiating in all directions, energetic
suspended motion
```
Test 2-3 composition variants per product to find the winning layout.

## ⚙️ Step 2 — The Subject Preparation Checklist
Nano Banana produces best results when it has a clean product reference to work from.
### The Subject Photo Requirements
→ **Resolution**: 4K minimum<br>→ **Background**: Preferably plain white or transparent PNG. If not available, any clean single-color background.<br>→ **Angle**: Standard hero angle (3/4 view or straight front)<br>→ **Lighting**: Even lighting on the product. Avoid harsh shadows.<br>→ **Product state**: Product should be in its intended sales state (labels facing forward, cap on, in packaging if applicable)
### If You Don't Have a Clean Product Photo
Option A — Extract from existing photography:
1. Take your best existing product photo
2. Use [Remove.bg](http://remove.bg/) or Photoshop to isolate the product on transparent background
3. Save at 4K resolution
4. Use as reference
Option B — Quick capture:
1. Place product on white surface (paper, table, cardboard)
2. Even lighting from front + slight fill
3. Shoot on phone at 4K
4. Take 3-4 angles
5. Pick cleanest for reference
Option C — Use Nano Banana to reproduce your product:
1. Take any product photo
2. Prompt Nano Banana: "Reproduce this exact product on plain white background, product photography aesthetic, 4K"
3. Get back a cleaner reference to use in the hero shot prompt

## ⚙️ Step 4 — The Seedance 2.0 Still-to-Video Animation Prompt
Once Nano Banana produces a levitating still, run 1 more prompt in Seedance 2.0 to turn it into an animated hero video. This is the killer upgrade.
### The Animation Prompt
```javascript
Animate this still image into a 5-second hero
video. Preserve exact composition, product
identity, and aesthetic of the source image.
Add subtle motion:

→ Levitating pieces gently rotate + drift with
  realistic physics
→ Scattered ingredient_bits fall in slow-motion,
  passing behind and in front of the hero
  product
→ Subtle atmospheric particles (dust or shimmer)
  drift through the composition
→ Camera: slow gentle push-in from wide to
  medium, 6 inches over the 5 seconds
→ Lighting: hold consistent with source image,
  slight rim light shift as camera moves

Physics rules:
→ Heavier elements drift downward slowly
→ Lighter elements float upward or sideways
→ All elements have realistic weight + momentum
→ No sudden movements — everything smooth and
  intentional

Duration: 5 seconds
Aspect ratio: match source image
Resolution: 4K
```
### Running the Animation
1. Open Higgsfield or Replicate → select Seedance 2.0 Pro
2. Attach the Nano Banana still image as reference
3. Paste the animation prompt above
4. Set output: 5 seconds, 4K, matching aspect ratio
5. Render
Render time: 3-6 minutes.
Cost: \\~$3-$5 per animated hero video.
### What Seedance Produces
A 5-second video with your Nano Banana still image now animated: pieces drift with physics, scatter falls in slow-motion, camera slowly pushes in, atmospheric particles enhance the premium aesthetic.
The still image goes on your landing page / packaging. The animated video goes on Meta / TikTok / brand site autoplay hero.
Same aesthetic, both formats, both derived from the same 1 source prompt structure.

## ⚙️ What NOT to Do
→ Don't skip the reference product photo. Nano Banana needs a subject reference for clean output.
→ Don't over-specify the [ingredient_bits]. 2-3 elements maximum. More than 3 = cluttered.
→ Don't use the master prompt as-is without filling variables. The "[subject]" and "[ingredient_bits]" placeholders must be replaced.
→ Don't animate every still. Some hero images are best as stills only (landing pages / packaging). Reserve animation for social / autoplay deploys.
→ Don't skip the composition variants. Test 2-3 (stacked / orbital / cascade / explosion) per product. You won't know which composition wins for your product until you test.
→ Don't run animations shorter than 3s or longer than 8s. 5s is optimal.
→ Don't add loud music to animated hero videos. Premium feel = minimal or no music.
→ Don't ignore the brand overlay step. Raw Nano Banana output is beautiful but generic. Brand overlay is what makes it your brand's asset.
→ Don't use the Nano Banana output for direct-response ads without adapting the messaging. Premium hero = brand positioning content. Direct-response requires different overlay + CTA treatment.
→ Don't skip the ingredient_bits scatter. Removing the scatter kills the premium editorial feel. It's not optional.
→ Don't run this format on low-resolution reference photos. Sub-4K source = drift in Nano Banana generation.
→ Don't try to VFX or heavily modify Nano Banana output. Take what it gives you. Curate. Don't compound editing.
