## Before You Start: Setup
### Upload Your Product
**Option A:** Paste your product URL. Marketing Studio scrapes details, images, and description automatically.

## Creator Type #1 — The Testimonial Reviewer
**Replaces:** The $1,500-$3,000 creator you hire to film a genuine product review.
**What makes this creator type effective:**
- Direct-to-camera authenticity
- Specific personal story, not generic praise
- Calm, conversational pacing
- Single camera angle, minimal editing
- Feels like advice from a friend
*[📸 IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Example testimonial output screenshot]*
**Platform:** Instagram Feed, Facebook Feed
**Aspect ratio:** 1:1 or 4:5
**Length:** 15-20 seconds
```javascript
Testimonial-style product review. Clean, bright background.
[AVATAR] seated on a couch in a naturally lit living room.
Soft daylight from a window. Slightly polished but still
casual and real.

Single camera angle throughout. No cuts. The simplicity
is what makes it feel authentic.

(0-3s) HOOK: She looks at camera with a thoughtful
expression. "I've been using [PRODUCT NAME] for
[TIMEFRAME] now and here's my honest take."
Tone: calm, like she's telling a friend.

(3-8s) STORY: "I started because I was dealing with
[SPECIFIC PAIN POINT]. I'd already tried [ALTERNATIVE 1]
and [ALTERNATIVE 2] and neither worked for me because
[SPECIFIC REASON THEY FAILED]."

(8-14s) RESULT: She picks up [YOUR PRODUCT], holds it
naturally. "After [TIMEFRAME] with [PRODUCT NAME],
[SPECIFIC MEASURABLE RESULT]. I didn't change anything
else. I just added this to my [ROUTINE]."

(14-18s) RECOMMENDATION: Sets product down, looks
directly at camera. "If you're dealing with [PAIN POINT],
just try it. I wish I'd found it sooner."

Conversational tone throughout. No selling. No urgency.
Just a person sharing what worked for them.
Natural room audio, soft ambient sound.
```
**Hook variations (swap into the 0-3s section):**
- "Nobody asked but I need to talk about [PRODUCT NAME]"
- "Okay so I almost didn't post this but [PRODUCT NAME] actually changed my [ROUTINE]"
- "I'm not someone who recommends products but [PRODUCT NAME] is different"
- "3 months with [PRODUCT NAME]. Here's what actually happened."
- "I keep getting asked about my [ROUTINE] so here it is"
**Why this works:** Single camera angle with zero cuts is the hallmark of authentic testimonial content. It feels like FaceTime with a friend, not a produced ad. The "honest take" hook disarms viewer skepticism immediately.

## Creator Type #2 — The Unboxing Creator
**Replaces:** The $500-$1,500 creator you ship product to for an unboxing video.
**What makes this creator type effective:**
- Overhead camera angle (hands and product only)
- ASMR-quality audio (package sounds, product sounds)
- Genuine first reactions and impressions
- Anticipation and reveal psychology
- Triggers dopamine through the "opening a gift" sensation
*[📸 IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Example unboxing output screenshot]*
**Platform:** TikTok, Instagram Reels
**Aspect ratio:** 9:16
**Length:** 15 seconds
```javascript
UGC unboxing video. Overhead camera angle looking
straight down at a clean desk or table surface.
Natural daylight from a window to the left.
Warm, inviting tones.

(0-3s) HOOK: Hands pull a branded shipping box into
frame from below. Voiceover with genuine excitement:
"This just arrived and I've been waiting for this
all week."

(3-6s) OPEN: Hands carefully open the outer packaging.
Show branded tape, tissue paper, any premium inserts.
ASMR focus on the sounds: tape peeling, paper
crinkling, box opening. Voiceover: "Okay the
packaging alone is worth it."

(6-10s) REVEAL: Hands lift [YOUR PRODUCT] out of the
box. Hold it up slightly so overhead camera catches
the detail and label. Slight pause for the reveal
moment. Voiceover: "So this is [PRODUCT NAME] and
it's supposed to [ONE KEY BENEFIT]."

(10-15s) FIRST TOUCH: Hands interact with the product
naturally. Open it, feel the texture, smell it,
read the ingredients. Voiceover: "The [TEXTURE/WEIGHT
/QUALITY] is actually really impressive. Full review
coming after I use it for a week."

ASMR-quality audio throughout. Every package crinkle,
every product sound captured. Overhead angle never
changes. Warm, soft lighting. No face on camera.
```
**Hook variations:**
- "[BRAND] just sent me something and I need to open it right now"
- "Unboxing the [PRODUCT] that sold out 3 times"
- "I ordered this at 2am and I don't regret it"
- "Package day is the best day. Let's see what's inside."
- "Everyone's been talking about [PRODUCT NAME]. Let's see if it's worth the hype."
**Why this works:** Overhead camera with hands-only is the most-watched unboxing format on TikTok. ASMR audio doubles average watch time. The "full review coming" creates a follow-back incentive without a hard sell.

## Creator Type #3 — The Lifestyle Creator
**Replaces:** The $2,000-$3,500 creator who produces aspirational content integrating your product into a beautiful daily routine.
**What makes this creator type effective:**
- Zero dialogue. The visuals sell the feeling.
- Cinematic production quality
- Product appears naturally in a desirable context
- The viewer projects themselves into the scene
- Elevates perceived product value through setting
*[📸 IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Example lifestyle output screenshot]*
**Platform:** Instagram Feed, Pinterest, Instagram Reels
**Aspect ratio:** 4:5 (feed) or 9:16 (reels)
**Length:** 15 seconds
```javascript
Lifestyle video showing [YOUR PRODUCT] naturally
integrated into a morning routine. Cinematic film
tone, 35mm, warm golden hour color palette.

No dialogue. No voiceover. Let the visuals do
everything. Background: soft ambient music, natural
sounds (coffee pouring, birds, fabric rustling).

(0-4s) SCENE: Wide shot of a sunlit kitchen or
bathroom. Morning light streaming through windows.
[AVATAR] walks in wearing comfortable, stylish
clothes. Slow, relaxed movements. She looks content.

(4-8s) NATURAL USE: Medium shot. She reaches for
[YOUR PRODUCT] from the counter or shelf as a natural
part of her routine. Uses it without looking at
camera. Not performative. Just a person in their
morning.

(8-12s) DETAIL: Close-up of [YOUR PRODUCT] being
used. Shallow depth of field. Warm light catching
the texture, the label, the color. The product
looks premium and inviting in this setting.

(12-15s) MOMENT: Medium shot. She pauses. Takes a
breath. Looks out the window or closes her eyes for
a moment. The feeling of calm is the message.
Hold for 2 seconds. End.

Warm tone, amber tinted, soft film grain.
Smooth gimbal movements throughout.
No text overlays. No CTA. Pure feeling.
```
**Variations to test:**
- Swap the setting: bathroom morning routine, evening wind-down, weekend breakfast
- Swap the season: summer morning light vs. cozy winter lighting with candles
- Swap the music mood: upbeat morning energy vs. calm meditative
**Why this works:** Lifestyle content sells the feeling, not the features. No voiceover forces the viewer to project themselves into the scene. Premium production value elevates perceived product value. This format performs best on Instagram feed and Pinterest where aspirational content drives saves and shares.

## Creator Type #4 — The Before & After Creator
**Replaces:** The $1,000-$2,500 creator who films transformation content showing your product's visible results.
**What makes this creator type effective:**
- Visual proof does the selling
- The contrast between states is undeniable
- Highest-converting format for transformation products
- "Seeing is believing" psychology
- Works for skincare, fitness, cleaning, organizing, hair, supplements
*[📸 IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Example before/after output screenshot]*
**Platform:** Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, TikTok
**Aspect ratio:** 1:1 (feed) or 9:16 (TikTok/Reels)
**Length:** 15 seconds
```javascript
Before and after product demonstration. Two clearly
different moments showing visible transformation.

(0-3s) BEFORE: [AVATAR] looking frustrated, tired,
or disappointed. Lighting is slightly flat, cooler
tones. She shows the "before" state directly to
camera. "This was me [TIMEFRAME] ago.
[DESCRIBE THE VISIBLE PROBLEM]."

(3-5s) TRANSITION: Quick cut. Screen briefly flashes
white or uses a swipe transition. Text appears:
"[X WEEKS] LATER..."

(5-10s) AFTER: Same person, same setting. But now
the lighting is warmer, she looks confident and
happy. She shows the "after" result directly.
"This is me now. Same [routine/setup/life],
I just added [PRODUCT NAME]."

(10-13s) PROOF: Close-up of the specific change.
Side-by-side if possible. Let the viewer see the
difference without explanation. The visual proof
is the argument.

(13-15s) PRODUCT: She holds up [YOUR PRODUCT].
"[SPECIFIC METRIC OR VISIBLE CHANGE]. Link in bio."

Bright, clean lighting in both states but the
"after" should feel noticeably warmer and more
inviting. The contrast should be visible without
explanation.
```
**Hook variations:**
- "I can't believe this is the same [skin/hair/space/setup]"
- "[TIMEFRAME] apart. One product. Same everything else."
- "POV: you finally found the [PRODUCT TYPE] that works"
- "I almost didn't film the before. Glad I did."
- "Stop scrolling if you're dealing with [PROBLEM]. Watch this."
**Why this works:** Before/after is the highest-converting ad format for any product with a visible transformation. The visual contrast does the selling. The transition creates a dopamine hit. The "same everything else, just added this" framing isolates the product as the only variable.

## Creator Type #5 — The Comparison Creator
**Replaces:** The $1,000-$2,000 creator who films side-by-side product comparisons positioning your product against competitors.
**What makes this creator type effective:**
- Positions your product against a known reference point
- The viewer sees the difference, they don't have to take your word
- "I tested it so you don't have to" builds trust
- Works because people are already comparing products before buying
- The creator's verdict carries weight because they showed the work
*[📸 IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Example comparison output screenshot]*
**Platform:** YouTube, Facebook, TikTok
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (YouTube) or 9:16 (TikTok)
**Length:** 15-20 seconds
```javascript
Product comparison video. Clean desk setup with two
products placed side by side. Good overhead lighting.
Clean, neutral background.

[AVATAR] sits behind the desk, speaks directly to
camera throughout.

(0-3s) HOOK: "I compared [YOUR PRODUCT] to
[COMPETITOR OR GENERIC ALTERNATIVE] so you don't
have to." Direct eye contact. Confident tone.
Picks up both products, one in each hand.

(3-8s) SETUP: Shows each product to camera.
"Most people use [COMPETITOR/GENERIC]. It costs
[PRICE] and does [WHAT IT CLAIMS]. [YOUR PRODUCT]
costs [PRICE] and claims [KEY DIFFERENTIATOR]."
Sets both down side by side.

(8-14s) TEST: Demonstrates both products. Shows
the visible or measurable difference in real time.
"[YOUR PRODUCT]: [SPECIFIC SUPERIOR RESULT].
[COMPETITOR]: [SPECIFIC INFERIOR RESULT]." Close-up
inserts of each product during the test.

(14-18s) VERDICT: Pushes [YOUR PRODUCT] forward,
moves competitor aside. "If you're still using
[COMPETITOR/GENERIC], you're wasting your money.
[YOUR PRODUCT]. Every time." Taps the product
twice for emphasis.

Clean audio, well-lit desk. Product-focused camera
angles with occasional close-up inserts during the
test. Confident, slightly opinionated tone. Not
angry, just certain.
```
**Hook variations:**
- "[YOUR PRODUCT] vs [COMPETITOR]: one clear winner"
- "Everyone recommends [COMPETITOR]. They're wrong. Here's why."
- "I spent $[TOTAL] testing [CATEGORY] products. This one won."
- "Your [COMPETITOR] is costing you [MONEY/TIME/RESULTS]. Here's proof."
- "I switched from [COMPETITOR] to [YOUR PRODUCT] [TIMEFRAME] ago. Not going back."
**Why this works:** Comparison content positions your product against a known reference point. The viewer doesn't have to take your word for it because they see the difference. The "so you don't have to" hook positions the creator as doing the work on the viewer's behalf. The physical gesture of pushing the winner forward creates a visual verdict.

## The Testing Framework
You have 5 creator types. Here's how to find which ones convert for your product.
**Week 1: Generate all 5**
One video per creator type. Same product. Same key benefit. Different format.
**Week 1-2: Launch with equal budget**
$15-$25 per video. Equal spend. Let the data decide.
**After $20-$30 per ad, check:**
<table header-row="true">
<tr>
<td>Metric</td>
<td>Kill</td>
<td>Keep</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Hook rate (3s views / impressions)</td>
<td>Below 25%</td>
<td>Above 35%</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Hold rate (50%+ watched)</td>
<td>Below 15%</td>
<td>Above 25%</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>CTR</td>
<td>Below 0.8%</td>
<td>Above 1.5%</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>CPA</td>
<td>2x+ above target</td>
<td>At or below target</td>
</tr>
</table>
**Week 2: Kill bottom 2-3. Scale survivors.**
Double budget on the top 2-3 formats.
**Week 3: Iterate on winners.**
Take your winning creator type and generate 10 variations:
- 3 different hooks
- 2 different avatars
- Different background/setting variations
- Platform-specific versions (TikTok cut vs. Instagram cut)
The first 5 ads find the format. The next 20 variations find the message.
