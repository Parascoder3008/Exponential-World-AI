## Step 1 — Set Up Higgsfield With Your Product URL
Open Higgsfield Marketing Studio. Paste your product URL into the URL field. Click generate.
Higgsfield reads your landing page, scrapes the Meta Ads Library and TikTok Creative Center for proven hooks in your category, and returns a starting kit:
→ A library of suggested AI personas (face + voice) that fit your category<br>→ 3-5 winning hook formats based on what's currently performing in your niche<br>→ A reference image showing your product on screen, framed as a UGC scene<br>→ Format options: 9:16 (TikTok/Reels), 1:1 (Instagram Feed), 16:9 (YouTube)
### What to feed it:
→ Direct product page URL (not the homepage — go to the actual product)<br>↳ Higgsfield reads the product photos, headline, and benefit copy.
→ Specify your goal in the brief field<br>↳ "30-second UGC ad for cold traffic on TikTok" works. Don't leave it blank.
→ Add 1 or 2 ICP descriptors<br>↳ "Women 28-45, busy moms, premium-curious." The persona suggestions sharpen.
### What you'll get back:
A dashboard with persona thumbnails, hook options, and a preview of the scene. Skim it. Don't perfect it yet — Step 2 is where you lock the persona.

### The persona lock rules:
→ Pick once per campaign. Reuse across all variations.<br>↳ Persona switching mid-campaign breaks the recognition loop.
→ Match face energy to product price.<br>↳ Premium product = more polished persona. Mass market = more casual persona.
→ Voice must match face.<br>↳ Don't pair a 25-year-old face with a 50-year-old voice. Higgsfield's voice library auto-suggests but verify before locking.
→ Pin your top 3 personas per ICP.<br>↳ When you launch the next campaign, you skip the search step.
### Custom persona prompt template (when stock library doesn't fit):
```javascript

[SKIN TONE], [EYE COLOR], [MAKEUP LEVEL], wearing

CONFIDENT/CALM], [SLOW/MEDIUM/QUICK] pace, slight
[REGIONAL ACCENT IF ANY]. Friendly and natural,
not actor-like.
```
Lock every detail. Empty fields = drift across regenerations.

## Step 3 — Prompt Seedance 2.0 With Screenshot-On-Screen
This is where the ad gets its punch.
The screenshot-on-screen technique: the persona holds a phone showing your actual product page or app screenshot. The viewer sees a real person reacting to your real product — not a generic AI scene.
### The Seedance master prompt:
```javascript
A [PERSONA from @Image1, maintain exact face, hair,
skin, makeup, wardrobe across all frames] holding a
phone at chest level. The phone screen displays
[YOUR PRODUCT SCREENSHOT — ATTACH AS @Image2].

(0-3s) HOOK: Persona looks at phone, eyes widen with
genuine surprise. Camera: handheld selfie angle with
subtle natural shake. Dialogue from @Audio1:
"[HOOK LINE FROM HOOK BANK]"

(3-8s) PROBLEM: Persona lowers phone slightly,
looks at camera. Camera: slow push-in to medium
close-up. Dialogue: "[PAIN POINT WITH SPECIFICITY]"

(8-13s) SOLUTION: Persona lifts phone again,
points at the screen with index finger to highlight
[SPECIFIC FEATURE]. Camera: rack focus from
persona's face to the phone screen. Dialogue:
"[BENEFIT WITH NUMBER OR SPECIFIC RESULT]"

(13-15s) CTA: Persona looks back at camera with
relaxed smile, phone still in frame. Camera: hold
static. Dialogue: "[SOFT CTA — link in bio, app
store, etc.]"

Constraints: maintain face consistency, avoid
identity drift, sharp focus on the phone screen,
phone screen text must remain legible across all
frames, natural hand movement, no extra fingers,
no bent limbs, no jitter, natural colors, stable
picture.
```
### Why this prompt works:
→ The screenshot-on-screen anchors the ad in your actual product<br>↳ Generic AI ads get scrolled past. Real product on real screen stops the scroll.
→ Camera direction per section<br>↳ Without specific camera moves, Seedance defaults to flat locked-off shots that look like AI.
→ Constraints lock the phone screen content<br>↳ Without "phone screen text must remain legible," Seedance turns your screenshot into blurry pixels.
### Where to get the phone screenshot:
→ Open your product page on a real iPhone<br>→ Take a screenshot<br>→ Upload as @Image2 in Seedance
Don't use a desktop screenshot resized down. The phone-native aspect ratio renders correctly. Desktop screenshots warp.

## Step 4 — Chain Higgsfield + Seedance for Face-Consistent UGC
Higgsfield's persona output is your starting frame. Seedance animates it. The chain is what holds the face across the full ad.
### The chain workflow:
1. Generate the persona reference image in Higgsfield (Step 1+2)
2. Download the persona image as a PNG
3. Upload to Seedance as @Image1
4. Upload your product screenshot as @Image2
5. Generate the voice in ElevenLabs (or use Higgsfield's voice library)
6. Upload the audio as @Audio1
7. Run the Seedance prompt from Step 3
8. Output ships in 9:16
### For 1:1 and 16:9 versions:
Re-run the same prompt in Seedance with the aspect ratio swap. Same @Image1, same @Image2, same @Audio1.
→ 9:16 for TikTok and Instagram Reels<br>→ 1:1 for Instagram Feed and Facebook Feed<br>→ 16:9 for YouTube and Facebook in-stream
3 generations from the same inputs. 3 different platform-native ads.
### Face consistency rules:
→ Always reference @Image1 in every prompt<br>↳ Without it, the model regenerates the face from scratch each time.
→ Reuse the same @Audio1 across all 3 aspect ratios<br>↳ Same voice across platforms = brand recognition.
→ For multi-shot ads (over 15 seconds), chain extensions<br>↳ Generate the first 15s. Upload as @Video1. Prompt the next 15s with "Extend @Video1, maintain @Image1 identity, same @Audio1 voice."
### When to regenerate vs. accept:
→ Face changes slightly between cuts → regenerate the offending clip<br>→ Phone screen turns into blurry text → add stronger constraint, regenerate<br>→ Voice tone shifts → regenerate audio in ElevenLabs, re-upload<br>→ Hand goes weird on the phone → regenerate (this is the most common issue)

## 5 Starter Templates
Each one fills in 5 minutes. Copy, swap the placeholders, generate.
### Template 1 — Direct response UGC (cold traffic)
**Goal:** Stop the scroll, deliver the hook, drive the click.
**Best platform:** TikTok, Instagram Reels (9:16)
**Persona:** Casual female 25-32, polished but relatable
**Hook script:**
```javascript
(0-3s) "Nobody told me about [PRODUCT] and I'm
honestly mad."

(3-8s) "I've been using [PRODUCT CATEGORY] for
[TIME] and they all do [COMMON FAILURE]. This one
doesn't."

(8-13s) "[SPECIFIC RESULT WITH NUMBER]. Linked
in my bio."

(13-15s) "[SOFT CTA — go check it]."
```
### Template 2 — Founder POV (mid-funnel)
**Goal:** Build trust through founder authenticity.
**Best platform:** Instagram Feed, LinkedIn (1:1 or 16:9)
**Persona:** Late 20s-30s, slightly less polished, founder energy
**Hook script:**
```javascript
(0-4s) "I built [PRODUCT] because I was tired of
[PROBLEM IN CATEGORY]."

(4-10s) "Here's what makes it different:
[KEY DIFFERENTIATOR WITH SPEC]."

(10-18s) "We've had [NUMBER] people use it in
[TIMEFRAME]. The feedback we get most:
[CUSTOMER QUOTE]."

(18-22s) "Try it. Link's in the post."
```
### Template 3 — Comparison ad (BOF / consideration)
**Goal:** Convert the comparison shopper.
**Best platform:** Facebook Feed, YouTube pre-roll (1:1 or 16:9)
**Persona:** Confident reviewer, late 20s-40s, opinionated tone
**Hook script:**
```javascript
(0-3s) "I tested [YOUR PRODUCT] against [COMPETITOR
1] and [COMPETITOR 2]."

(3-9s) "[COMPETITOR 1] does [WEAKNESS]. [COMPETITOR 2]
does [WEAKNESS]."

(9-16s) "[YOUR PRODUCT] does [DIFFERENTIATOR WITH
SPEC]. That's why I switched."

(16-22s) "[CTA — see the comparison in my bio]."
```
### Template 4 — Day-in-the-life integration (TOF awareness)
**Goal:** Build category interest, not direct conversion.
**Best platform:** Instagram Feed, Pinterest (4:5 or 1:1)
**Persona:** Aspirational lifestyle, 28-40, polished
**Hook script:**
```javascript
(0-5s) Visual only — persona using product as part of
morning routine

(5-12s) "Three things I do every morning. [PRODUCT]
is one of them."

(12-18s) "[SPECIFIC USE CASE WITH BENEFIT]."

(18-22s) "Quietly the most-used thing in my house."
```
### Template 5 — Problem-solution narrative (BOF)
**Goal:** Convert the problem-aware shopper.
**Best platform:** Facebook, Instagram (9:16 or 1:1)
**Persona:** Relatable everyday user, 25-40
**Hook script:**
```javascript
(0-4s) "Every [TARGET] knows this: [SPECIFIC PAIN
POINT]."

(4-9s) "I tried [COMPETITOR 1]. Tried [COMPETITOR 2].
Then [PRODUCT]."

(9-16s) "[NUMBER] [TIMEFRAME] later: [SPECIFIC
RESULT]."

(16-20s) "[CTA — link below]."
```
